Getting started

QHow do I sign in or get an account?

Your firm’s admin invites you by email. Click the invite, set a password, and you’ll land in your firm’s private workspace. Nothing to install — AquiferOps runs in your web browser on a computer, phone, or tablet.

QI didn’t get my invitation email.

Check your spam or junk folder and search for “AquiferOps.” If it’s still missing, your firm’s admin can re-send the invite — and if the link is old it may have expired, so ask for a fresh one.

QI forgot my password.

Use the “Forgot password” link on the sign-in screen to reset it by email. Be sure to use the same email address your invite was sent to.

QI’m signed in but the screen looks empty or wrong.

You’re likely in the wrong workspace. Look at the workspace switcher in the top-right corner and switch to your firm. Each firm’s data is private to its own workspace, so nothing crosses over.

QWhere do I start? What’s the first thing to do?

A simple first path: add a client (under Work), create a project for them, add a site and its wells (under Field), bring in your data, then open the site to see your first analysis. During early access we’ll walk your team through it.

Bringing in your data

QHow do I get my lab results in?

Import your lab’s electronic data file into the project. The first time a lab’s format is new, you match its columns once and AquiferOps remembers it. As results load, each one is automatically checked against the binding limits and any exceedances are flagged.

QMy lab file won’t import.

Usually it’s an unsupported file type or a missing required column. Double-check the file, and if it still won’t go, send it to our team rather than forcing a workaround — we’ll make sure it loads correctly.

QHow do I enter a “non-detect” result?

Enter it as a non-detect — don’t substitute a made-up number like zero. AquiferOps handles non-detects correctly in the statistics using accepted methods (Kaplan-Meier and ROS), so entering them honestly keeps your numbers defensible.

QMy field readings aren’t showing up.

Field mode works offline and syncs when the device is back in signal. Make sure the device that recorded the readings has reconnected so the sync can finish — and don’t re-enter them elsewhere first, or you may create duplicates.

Analysis & results

QWhy don’t I see a trend for an analyte yet?

A trend needs enough sampling events over time to be meaningful. With only one or two results there isn’t yet enough data to draw a trend — add the missing sampling events and it will populate.

QAn exceedance flag looks wrong to me.

First check that the value, units, and analyte name are entered correctly — a unit mismatch is the usual culprit. Whether a flagged result is truly a regulatory exceedance, and what to do about it, is a professional judgment call — and our team is glad to talk it through with you.

QWhich regulations does it check my data against?

It’s built around the federal EPA (SW-846) and ASTM standards used nationwide, with state-specific limits configurable. We’re getting a few states deeply right first — tell us yours and we’ll share where it stands before you rely on the flags.

QCan I trust the math?

The methods are the published, standard ones the field already trusts — Mann-Kendall with Sen’s slope for trends, Kaplan-Meier and ROS for non-detects, ASTM D2487 / USCS for soils, EPA SW-846 for holding times — not black-box approximations. Every result traces back to its source data.

QA holding time shows as overdue — what does that mean?

The holding-time clock counts each sample against its lab limit (EPA SW-846 / state). “Overdue” means a sample has passed that limit, and your dashboard surfaces it under “What needs you today.” What to do about it is a professional decision — our team can help you think it through.

Reports & sharing

QMy report is missing figures or tables.

Reports assemble from your project data, so a missing figure usually means the underlying data isn’t in yet or isn’t marked ready. Add or approve the underlying data, then rebuild the report.

QThe report format doesn’t match what my regulator wants.

Reports are built to a regulator-ready structure, and templates can be tailored to your firm. If your state or program needs a specific layout, tell us the exact requirement and we’ll get it set up.

QHow do I share a project with my client?

The client portal gives each client a secure, read-only view of their own project — approved deliverables only, by a private link, scoped to the projects you choose, and revocable anytime. It’s included in every plan at no extra cost.

QHow do I get my data or reports back out?

Reports download as finished files, and your records and accounting export to spreadsheet (CSV). You own your data and can take it with you anytime.

Account & privacy

QWhat does it cost, and how do I change my plan?

Pricing is per user, per month, with a free trial and no setup fee. See the pricing page for current tiers, and manage plan changes in your account settings. During early access, reach out and we’ll get you sorted.

QIs my data private and secure?

Yes. Each firm’s data is kept private to that firm, hosted on secure, industry-standard cloud infrastructure, and never sold or shared. You stay the owner of your data.

QWhat happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export your records and reports at any time (files and CSV), so your work is always yours to keep.
