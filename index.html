Questions firms ask

Straight answers to what consultants actually ask — tap any question.

QIs my data private and secure?

Yes. Each firm’s data is kept private to that firm, hosted on secure, industry-standard cloud infrastructure, and never sold or shared. You stay the owner of your data.

QCan I get my data back out?

Always. Reports download as finished files, and your records and accounting can be exported to spreadsheet (CSV). Your data is yours to take with you.

QWhich regulations does it support?

AquiferOps is built around the federal EPA (SW-846) and ASTM standards used nationwide, with state-specific limits configurable. We’re getting a few states deeply right first — tell us yours and we’ll share where it stands.

QHow hard is it to switch from my current tools?

You can import lab results and data-logger files with guided column-matching, so you’re not starting from scratch — and during early access we’ll help your team get set up.

QDo I need a signal in the field?

Not for water-level rounds — those work fully offline and sync when you’re back in range. Other field entry needs a connection.

QWho is AquiferOps for?

Small and mid-size environmental and hydrogeology consulting firms — from independent consultants to multi-team practices — that handle field data, lab results, the science, and regulator-ready reporting.
